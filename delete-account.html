Delete your GlobalTalks account

GlobalTalks (by GlobalTalks, Bangalore, India) lets you delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the GlobalTalks app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email support@globaltalksapp.com from your registered contact with the subject "Delete my account" and the phone number used to sign up. We will verify it is you and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login account and phone number
  • Your profile (name, photo) and settings
  • Your status updates and uploaded documents
  • Your Mom AI reminders and voice settings

What may remain

  • Messages you already sent stay in the other person's chat, as with any messaging app.
  • Payment records may be kept as required by Indian tax and accounting law. These are held by our payment provider, Razorpay.
  • Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle, so deleted data leaves backups within a limited period.

Deletion cannot be undone. Subscriptions are not refunded automatically on deletion.
